Bhaskar Ray
PhD Student · Department of Statistics · North Carolina State University

I am a fourth year PhD candidate in the Department of Statistics, North Carolina State University. I am currently working on collaborative learning methods, advised by Dr. Srijan Sengupta. My research lies at the intersection of statistical inference and optimization methods.
The general theme of my research is to understand the interplay of statistical uncertainty and optimization error in algorithms used for statistical estimation and inference. My dissertation primarily focused on methods like Federated Learning, used for privacy-aware estimation in a variety of statistical models. Besides, during my PhD journey, I have been collaborating closely with researchers from diverse fields, including neuroscience, chemical engineering, computer science and psychology on other projects.
