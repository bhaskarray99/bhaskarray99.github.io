Dissertation Project
PACE: Privacy Aware Collaborative Estimation for Heterogenous GLMs
We propose a novel two-step method of parameter estimation for a broad class of Generalized Linear Models under a distributed data framework with privacy restrictions on data sharing. Participating sites obtain data from the same distribution, the true parameter values varying across clusters. We conduct a hypothesis test-type clustering and subsequently, a decentralized collaborative estimation and compare the convergence and accuracy guarantees to local estimation. R package sgd was modified as sgditer for implementation of proposed method.
Federated Learning, Heterogeneity Collaborators: Dr. Srijan Sengupta, Dr. Aritra Mitra
Dissertation Project
Asymptotic Distribution of Averaged Federated Estimator with GLMs
We aim to quantify the asymptotic distribution of the FL approximation to the global MLE for GLMs, accounting for statistical uncertainty and optimization error.
Federated Learning, Asymptotics Collaborators: Dr. Srijan Sengupta, Dr. Aritra Mitra
Interdisciplinary Collaboration
A gut-brain interaction controlling reward learning
We are working on the statistical analysis of the timescale over which gut-derived signals based on food content modulate dopamine release, and how they interact with dopamine signals previously identified as critical for food reward learning and motivation.
Neuroscience Collaborators: Dr. William Howe, Dr. Srijan Sengupta, Tung Bui
